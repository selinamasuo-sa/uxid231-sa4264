> Project Overview <

The Metalheads founder and I were talking about implementing a product-on-demand (POD) system for Carver Robotics club merch. This would ensure that we wouldn't overspend and end up breaking even or losing money on merch like we had done in years prior.

> The Problem <

In previous years, ordering merchandise could leave us with excess inventory and money tied up in products that did not sell. We wanted a system that would allow us to offer club merchandise without having to purchase and maintain a large inventory beforehand.

> The Approach & Design Direction <

After a couple weeks of research and discussion, we settled on Fourthwall since they have one of the largest inventories of items we could use without purchasing a subscription. They also provide sample credits that can be used to order products for testing before officially launching them.

I started playing around with a demo site and found that they had sticker sheets available with customizable backgrounds.

From there, I started designing backgrounds to create a brand and eventually a wide selection of merch items for the FIRST Robotics club at Carver.

> Design Gallery <

> Outcome <

Although the proposal was reviewed but ultimately not implemented much beyond the promotional video, the assets did give the FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) team the initial social media momentum it needed as a rookie team. Additionally, they were able to create a custom Fourthwall site dedicated to their FRC team that they used to design and order custom t-shirts and zip-up hoodies!
