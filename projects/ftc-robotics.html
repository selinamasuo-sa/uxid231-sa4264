> Overview <

Throughout high school, I spent 3-4 years as a leading Tech Hero for Carver Engineering & Science's FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) program. My work covered programming, engineering portfolios, social media, videos, posters, logos, and other digital materials supporting both Carver FTC teams.

After graduating, I continued supporting the program as an alumni mentor. I work more closely with the teams on-site during Drexel's winter term, which overlaps with their kickoff and build season, while providing remote support throughout the off-season. I currently mentor both FTC teams as well as the newly established FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) team, the Metalheads.
