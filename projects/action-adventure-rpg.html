> Context <

The four of us (Youssef, Armaan, Elie, and I) decided to collaborate on this project for our first year CI10X sequence during our time as CS majors at Drexel's School of Computing and Information Sciences (SCIS), formerly called the College of Computing and Informatics (CCI). It is an action adventure RPG inspired by the Zelda and Pokemon series.

> Our Pitch <

"The open-world Zelda formula has been the same for nearly a decade. While Breath of the Wild was groundbreaking upon its 2017 release, recent games have drained its formula of all of its juice, leaving players thirsty for something fresh.

Our untitled free-to-play game draws inspiration from older Zelda entries as players explore an open world environment from a top-down view. Players can battle various fantasy enemies, interact with the environment in puzzling ways, and explore unique dungeons themed around regional biomes. By blending the classic Zelda formula with concepts from other modern RPGs, our game will throw a unique spin on the action-adventure genre."

> The Approach & Design Direction <

We learned how to use the Godot game engine to push and pull files and manage the project's assets and codebase. The map would function much like a typical Pokemon game on the Game Boy Advance where players could walk to the cardinal edges of a given scene to progress to a new sub-area of the map.

For a more detailed look at our approach and design direction, please refer to our final presentation video for the CI10X project.
