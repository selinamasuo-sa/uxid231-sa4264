> Project Overview <

This is the third volume of my FTC Bake Sale series.

> The Problem <

As a highschool robotics team, we often struggled with funding issues, especially when it came to supporting our various activities and competitions. There were some occassions when the district cut funding for many afterschool programs (including FTC) due to budget constraints. Running bake sales was one major way we circumvented this issue. Many other clubs faced similar struggles and also used bake sales as a solution.

> The Approach & Design Direction <

Our approach was to create a series of bake sales that stood out from the rest. We wanted to make each sale unique and memorable, while also generating enough revenue to support our team's activities. This volume had a retro theme that had food product highlights based on characters in videogames such as Cuphead and Pokemon. We also used bright neon colors to catch people's attention.

> Design Gallery <

> Outcome <

As the lead designer, I was very proud of the direction this project took, as we set a record for the highest revenue generated by a single bake sale in the history of the FTC program at our school!
