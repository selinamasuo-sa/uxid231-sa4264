FTC Robotics Bake Sale Posters: Vol. III

FTC Robotics

Project Overview

As the former Assistant Captiain and Digital Content Manager at my high school's (Carver Engineering and Science) FIRST Tech Challenge Robotics club, I was responsible for promoting our club digitally through our engineering portfolio and social media platforms. I primarily did this by posting bake sale flyers on our Instagram pages. The third iteration of this series was held on March 21st, 2024 where I assumed the role of lead graphic designer by designing the following posters and volunteered to serve goods to students.

Design Direction

During each iteration of bake sale promotion, I typically experiment with new methods of catching students' attention across the high school. This time around, I went with a retro theme, basing the food spotlights around characters in videogames such as Cuphead and Pokemon and using bright neon colors. This entire process was a ton of fun and we made a good amount of profit from it (even more so than our other two bake sales held earlier in the year).
