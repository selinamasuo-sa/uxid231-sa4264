It will showcase my works from various projects, such as web development, graphic and poster design, video editing, and other projects from my major-specific endeavors in computer science and computer engineering. I aim for the site to have a modern Zelda-themed aesthetic (i.e. Breath of the Wild, Tears of the Kingdom, Ocarina of Time on Nintendo Switch 2).
