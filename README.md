# Selinam Asuo

## About
I am Selinam Asuo, a Drexel student majoring in Computer Engineering and minoring in User Experience and
Interaction Design (UXID). I am currently in my second year and have an interest in graphic design, web
development, and user experience design, alongside my major's interests in creating and maintaing computer
software and hardware systems.

What initially drew me to computer engineering and computer science was my interest in video games and DIY
computers. One of my underclassmen classes in highschool required me to download Adobe Illustrator on my
old Dell Optiplex desktop that did not have a dedicated GPU, which made it impossible for me to work on
projects and assignments outside of the Macs in school. That inspired me to learn more about my Optiplex
and the parts that made the computer worked. I learned that older parts typically draw less power meaning
there wasn’t a need to have a huge power supply in them like many modern machines do. I eventually came
across an AMD Radeon RX 6400 GPU after learning that it didn’t draw much power and didn't need a dedicated
PCIe power cable, but had just power enough to run Illustrator. I felt great about this purchase and was
glad I put in the time and research necessary to make the decision. From there, I looked into getting a
laptop that could more efficiently complete the same tasks that my old desktop could. After spending months
looking at various videos from YouTubers such as Hardware Canucks and Jarrod’s Tech, I eventually settled
on my current laptop: an ASUS model with 16 gigabytes of memory, a Ryzen 7800HS CPU, and an Nvidia GeForce
RTX 3060 laptop GPU. This new ASUS laptop allowed me to complete my academic work in roughly half the time
as my old desktop while also allowing me to play games in my free time. That journey from barely knowing
anything about desktops to being somewhat knowledgeable in computer hardware and laptops is something
that I will never take for granted, and that I have been able to apply to many areas of my life.

From a programming perspective, that mostly started in highschool when I took Intro to CS and AP Computer
Science classes that taught me the basics of text-based languages like Python and Java; block-based
languages like Scratch and Snap!; web-based languages and tools like HTML, CSS, and Google Sites; and other
design based tools like Canva and Pixlr. I also learned Illustrator and InDesign through working on my
graduating class's highschool yearbook as a staff member and later as a copy editor. All of these experiences
culminated in me starting [a draft portfolio website on Wix that hosts my highschool projects](https://selinamasuo.wixsite.com/media).

Outside of my academic interests, I enjoy playing video games like Zelda and Super Smash Bros, as well as
baking, cooking, and going out to explore the city of Philadelphia with my friends. My favorite games are
Ocarina of Time and Breath of the Wild, and my favorite thing to bake is brownies! I make posters,
videos, and other graphic design work for my friends, family, personal projects, and both high school and
college organizations.

## Topic
This project focuses on creating a portfolio website that is responsive and mobile-friendly. It will
showcase my works from various projects, such as web development, graphic and poster design, video
editing, and other projects from my major-specific endeavors in computer science and computer engineering.
I aim for the site to have a modern Zelda-themed vibe (i.e. Breath of the Wild, Tears of the Kingdom,
Ocarina of Time on Nintendo Switch 2). I will be using [The Legend of Zelda series Nintendo page](https://www.nintendo.com/us/explore/characters/zelda/)
as loose reference for the aesthetic and design of the site. I plan to include content from [my Wix portfolio](https://selinamasuo.wixsite.com/media)
as well as other designs and projects I have worked on since then (since I have not updated that site
as frequently as I would have liked to).

## AI Use
This project will likely use AI tools (such as ChatGPT Edu and Claude) to assist in generating code
