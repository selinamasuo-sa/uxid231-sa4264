The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Memory Game

January 2023 - March 2023

This is a memory game that I created in highschool using Snap! by University of California, Berkeley. The game is based on the popular video game The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. The player uses a virtual ocarina to play and match songs that have randomly generated patterns, or songs directly from the game!

Carver FIRST Robotics Brand Overhaul Proposal

June 2025 - September 2025

This is a brand overhaul/product design side project I did for my highschool robotics team after I graduated. I was on one of their FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) teams up until I graduated, and I was part of the planning process for the FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) team that they planned to start up after my senior year. I am also a mentor of said FRC team. One idea that we had was to use Fouthwall, a product-on-demand (POD) service, for merch to prevent overspending, breaking even, or losing money on merch like we had done in years prior. I checked out some of the sticker sheets on their page, and proceeded to make this product design slideshow from there.
